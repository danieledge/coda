Before you unzip anything: copy everything on your CompactFlash card to your computer, and keep your netBook's own OS.IMG safe. Coda has been tried on a netBook with a serial device that behaves like a modem, and in an emulator, but not yet with a LAN (Ethernet) card. Read the install guide first.
